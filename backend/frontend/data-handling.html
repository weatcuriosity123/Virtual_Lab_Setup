Curiosity Lab / VMLabs

Data Handling Note

Institutional lab service · Last updated 22 October 2026

A practical guide for students, educators, and institution administrators using Curiosity Lab (VMLabs).

Temporary and saved work

  • Temporary home: download work you need before ending the lab. Session cleanup removes temporary desktop files.
  • Saved home: persistence requires an eligible classroom/template and an active storage entitlement. Files and settings under the saved home (/config) can survive new sessions. Unsaved edits, running processes, and files outside that home are not preserved by this feature.
  • Check the storage banner: it identifies temporary or saved storage and reports available quota information. A saved home is not a guarantee against data loss; keep an independent copy of important coursework.

Expiry, logout, and deletion are different

Ending a lab removes its running desktop. Logging out clears portal session credentials but does not end the container or pause its timer. Storage expiry and allowance changes apply to later sessions and do not automatically erase an existing saved home.

Authorized platform administrators can delete a selected saved home after attached containers are removed. That action has no dashboard undo. Separate backups and original migration copies require separate operator handling; secure media erasure and immediate deletion of every copy are not guaranteed.

Session activity and traffic captures

The platform receives session activity signals to manage inactivity limits and records operational session metadata. This is separate from a screen recording or transcript of every command. Applications within the desktop may keep their own histories or logs.

When you use Capture Traffic, the service creates a bounded capture of your lab’s network traffic for coursework. The portal reports the saved path; captures are temporary and a later capture replaces the previous one. Captures may contain sensitive network details or unencrypted payloads. Use only authorized lab traffic and review files before sharing or exporting them.

Safe use of a shared learning service

  • Use coursework or synthetic data. Avoid uploading confidential institutional records, other people’s personal data, production secrets, or sensitive personal documents unless your institution has expressly arranged appropriate handling.
  • Do not save personal passwords, API keys, or browser sign-in sessions inside a persistent home. Sign out of external services before leaving.
  • Keep desktop credentials and session links private. Do not include them in screenshots, feedback, or support requests.
  • End the lab when finished, then log out of the portal, especially on shared computers.

Institution and operator responsibilities

Institutions should tell users who administers their lab access and how to submit privacy requests. Host operators must manage access to the application database, stored homes, infrastructure logs, and backups. Backup copies can include student data and require their own retention and deletion process.

Hosting location, provider configuration, and institutional retention periods are deployment-specific. Ask your institution’s lab administrator for the arrangements that apply to your classroom; the application alone does not establish these terms.

Need help?

Contact your institution’s lab administrator or classroom instructor with your enrolled email, classroom, and a description of the issue. See the Privacy Policy for information categories and uses.
