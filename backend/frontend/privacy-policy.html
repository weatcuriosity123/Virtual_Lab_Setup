Curiosity Lab / VMLabs

Privacy Policy

Institutional lab service · Last updated 22 October 2026

This notice explains how Curiosity Lab (VMLabs) handles information when institutions provide browser-based Linux labs to students and educators. Read it together with your institution’s own privacy notice.

Information used by the service

  • Account and enrollment: your verified email address and account identifier, institution, classroom memberships, administrator roles, and lab or storage entitlements.
  • Lab operation: session identifiers, generated desktop credentials, classroom and template details, session status, timestamps, expiry, and activity signals used to operate sessions and apply limits.
  • Saved work: files and application settings in an enabled persistent home. These may include credentials you choose to save inside lab applications.
  • Support and administration: feedback ratings and comments, approval requests and decisions, and administrator activity records. Infrastructure logs may contain connection metadata such as IP addresses, requested URLs, timestamps, and errors.

Why information is used

The service uses this information to verify access, enroll users in classrooms, start and reconnect labs, manage storage and capacity, investigate faults or misuse, process institutional approvals, and review feedback.

Who can access it

Authorized institution administrators can access records exposed by their assigned college permissions. Platform administrators and host operators have broader access needed to operate the service, including technical access to stored files and backups. Lab storage should not be treated as a private vault inaccessible to operators.

Where Cloudflare Access and Tunnel are enabled, Cloudflare processes authentication and connection information. Your selected identity provider handles its sign-in process. External websites and services opened inside a lab receive information you send to them under their own policies.

Browser storage and sign-in

The portal uses browser session storage for session credentials and pending lab requests, and browser mechanisms to coordinate logout across tabs. Cloudflare Access uses its authentication cookies. The portal does not ask for your identity-provider password. Signing out of the portal does not necessarily sign you out of an identity provider or applications opened inside the desktop.

Retention and deletion

Temporary lab files are removed with session cleanup. Enabled persistent-home files can remain after a session ends. Storage expiry or access revocation does not itself delete an existing saved home. Deleting a saved home does not automatically remove separate backups or migration copies.

The application does not implement a universal automatic deletion schedule for account, session, feedback, audit, or saved-home records. Retention and backup disposal require the operator’s institutional process; this notice does not promise a fixed deletion period. See the Data Handling Note for practical details.

Questions and requests

Contact the institution’s lab administrator or the instructor who arranged your access for questions, corrections, access concerns, or deletion requests. Provide your enrolled email and classroom, but never send passwords, sign-in codes, or session credentials. Your institution can coordinate with the platform operator when host-level action is needed.

Changes to this notice

The revision date identifies this version. Changes to institutional arrangements or service data practices should be reflected in an updated notice.
